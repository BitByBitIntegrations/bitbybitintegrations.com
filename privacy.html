Privacy Policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

This policy covers All Platform Uploader, a tool made by Bit By Bit Integrations LLC (“we”). It posts a creator’s own short videos to their own YouTube, Facebook Page and TikTok accounts on a schedule they choose. It runs on the creator’s own computer and is used only by the people who connect their own accounts to it.

What the app accesses

  • Google Drive (read-only): the list of videos in the one folder you choose, and the video files themselves when it is time to upload them.
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to your channel and set their title, description and tags.
  • Instagram and Facebook (through Meta): your own Instagram reels’ captions, dates, links and video files, used to match each video to its caption, and permission to post Reels to the Facebook Page you connect.
  • TikTok (Login Kit and Content Posting API): your basic profile info (user.info.basic: open ID, display name, avatar) to show which account is connected; permission to send videos to your TikTok drafts (video.upload), where you add a sound, edit and post them yourself, since the app cannot publish on its own; and a read-only list of your public TikTok videos (video.list: captions, dates, lengths, links), used only so the app doesn't send you a draft of a video that's already on your TikTok.

How the data is used

Only to do what you set the app up to do: find your videos, pair each one with the caption you already wrote for it, and upload it to your other accounts on the schedule you pick. Videos are downloaded temporarily to your computer for uploading and deleted afterwards.

Where it is stored

Everything (login tokens, the list of videos and captions, upload history) is stored on your own computer. We do not run a server that collects it, and it is not sent anywhere except to the platforms you connected (Google, Meta, TikTok) when the app talks to them on your behalf.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising or to train any AI model.

All Platform Uploader’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

You can disconnect the app at any time from your Google account permissions, Facebook’s Business Integrations settings, or TikTok’s app permissions. Uninstalling the app from your computer deletes everything it stored. To ask us anything about your data, email bitbybitintegrationsllc@gmail.com.
