Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how All Platform Uploader (the “App”), made by Bit By Bit Integrations LLC (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use it. The App is a desktop tool that posts your own short videos to your own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and TikTok account on a schedule you choose. It runs on your own computer and works only with accounts you connect yourself.

1. Information the App accesses

The App only accesses what you authorize when you connect each account, and only for the purposes in section 2.

  • TikTok (through TikTok Login Kit, the Content Posting API and the Display API):
    • user.info.basic: your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar, to show which account is connected.
    • video.upload: permission to send your videos to your TikTok drafts (inbox). You review, edit, add a sound and post each draft yourself in the TikTok app. The App never publishes to TikTok on its own.
    • video.list: a read-only list of your public TikTok videos (caption, date, length, link), used only to avoid sending you a draft of a video that is already on your TikTok.
  • Google: read-only access to the one Google Drive folder you choose (the list of videos and the video files), and permission to upload videos to your YouTube channel and set their title, description, thumbnail and privacy setting.
  • Meta: your own Instagram posts’ captions, dates, links, cover images and video files, used to reuse captions you already wrote, and permission to post Reels to the Facebook Page you connect.
  • Notifications: if you turn this on, the App uses the Messages app on your computer to text your own phone number the caption for each TikTok draft.

The App does not access your TikTok messages, followers, likes, comments, analytics or any other account’s data, and it does not collect payment information, contacts or location.

2. How the information is used

  • to upload your videos to the accounts you connected, at the times you scheduled;
  • to reuse the captions you wrote, and to text them to you for your TikTok drafts;
  • to avoid duplicate posts, and to show you the status of each upload on the App’s dashboard.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, selling, or training any AI or machine-learning model, and we do not combine TikTok data with data from other sources except to pair your own videos with your own captions.

3. Where information is stored, and for how long

Everything the App handles (login tokens, the list of your videos and captions, upload history and logs) is stored only on your own computer, in the App’s local database. Bit By Bit Integrations LLC does not run a server that receives, stores or can see your data. Video files are downloaded to your computer only while they are being uploaded, then deleted. Your information stays on your computer until you disconnect an account or delete the App, which removes it.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your information with anyone. The App sends information only to the services you connected (TikTok, Google/YouTube, Meta), and only to carry out what you asked it to do, plus Apple’s Messages service if you turn on caption texts. Those services handle the information under their own policies: TikTok, Google, Meta, Apple. We may disclose information only if the law requires it.

The App’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.

5. Security

Data stays on your computer, and the App connects to each service over encrypted HTTPS using the official sign-in (OAuth) for each platform. The App never sees or stores your passwords. Keep your computer protected with a login password.

6. Your choices: disconnecting and deleting data

  • TikTok: remove the App in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions.
  • Google/YouTube: remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Meta: remove it in Facebook Settings → Business integrations.
  • Deleting the App from your computer deletes everything it stored. You can also email us to ask how to delete specific data, and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children and is not meant for anyone under 18, or the minimum age required by each connected platform. We do not knowingly process information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be noted on the App’s page.
