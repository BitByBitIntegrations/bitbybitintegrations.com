All Platform Uploader

A desktop tool by Bit By Bit Integrations LLC

All Platform Uploader helps a creator post their own short videos to their own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and TikTok account, on a schedule they choose. It runs on the creator's own computer, with a dashboard at localhost, and only works with accounts the creator connects themselves.

How it works

  1. The creator keeps their videos in a Google Drive folder and connects YouTube, their Facebook Page and TikTok from the dashboard.
  2. The app pairs each video with the caption the creator already wrote for it.
  3. At each scheduled time it uploads the next video: as a YouTube Short, as a Facebook Reel, and to the creator's TikTok drafts.
  4. For TikTok, the creator gets a notification with the caption, opens the draft in the TikTok app, adds a sound, pastes the caption and posts it themselves. The app never publishes to TikTok on its own.
  5. To avoid duplicates, the app reads the creator's list of public TikTok videos and skips any video that's already there.

TikTok features used

  • Login Kit (user.info.basic): the creator signs in with TikTok, and the dashboard shows which account is connected.
  • Content Posting API, upload to drafts (video.upload): sends the video to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft.
  • Display API (video.list): reads the creator's public videos so the app doesn't send a draft of something already posted.
